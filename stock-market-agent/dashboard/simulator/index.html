Stock Market Simulator

Practice how investing decisions look from six different seats — a retail investor, an investment banker sizing up a merger, a private banker managing several client books, a financial planner building goal-based plans, an options & futures trader using leverage and derivatives, and a short-selling buy-side investor running a long/short book.
This is a paper-trading education sandbox, not a real brokerage, and nothing in it is investment, legal or tax advice. Choose Real Companies (real tickers and real starting prices/metrics from the Market Scanner watchlist — always running in real time, with no fast-forward, so it can never be mistaken for a prediction or backtest of real market history) or Super Simulator (a fully fictional universe, speed adjustable from a day trader's 10 minutes per simulated year up to real time). Every price after the starting snapshot, in both modes, is generated by a disclosed random-walk model — not real market data — and will diverge from what the real security actually does. All cash, positions and plans are stored locally in your browser (nothing is sent anywhere) and reset if you clear site data. Do not use this tool, or anything in it, to make real investment decisions about real securities.

Before you start

This is a paper-trading education sandbox, not a real brokerage, and nothing in it is investment, legal or tax advice.

Real Companies mode starts from real tickers and real starting prices/metrics, and always runs in real time. But every price move after that starting point — in both Real Companies and Super Simulator modes — is generated by a disclosed random-walk simulation, not real market data. It will diverge from what the real security actually does, sometimes within minutes.

Never use this tool, or anything in it, to make real investment decisions about real securities. All data stays in your browser's local storage only.
