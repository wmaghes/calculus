Stock Market Simulator

Practice how investing decisions look from four different seats — a retail investor, a private banker managing several client books, a financial advisor building goal-based plans, and an investment banker sizing up a merger.
This is a paper-trading sandbox, not a real brokerage. Choose Real Companies (the Market Scanner watchlist, starting from real prices and metrics) or Super Simulator (a fully fictional universe) below, then pick how fast the market moves — from a day trader's 1 simulated year per 10 real minutes up to real-time. All cash, positions and plans are stored locally in your browser (nothing is sent anywhere) and reset if you clear site data. Nothing here is investment advice; it's a teaching tool for how each role thinks about risk, return and time horizon.
All figures are illustrative. Real Companies mode starts from real snapshot data but moves forward using a disclosed random-walk model calibrated from that snapshot — not a replay of actual historical prices. Super Simulator mode is entirely fictional. Not investment advice.
