Options & Futures Trader

You trade derivatives instead of shares: options (the right, not the obligation, to buy/sell at a fixed strike before expiry) and futures (a leveraged bet on a price, marked to market continuously). Both let you control far more exposure than your cash alone — and both can lose money faster than owning the stock outright.
This is a paper-trading education sandbox, not a real brokerage, and nothing in it is investment, legal or tax advice. Choose Real Companies (real tickers and real starting prices/metrics from the Market Scanner watchlist — always running in real time, with no fast-forward, so it can never be mistaken for a prediction or backtest of real market history) or Super Simulator (a fully fictional universe, speed adjustable from a day trader's 10 minutes per simulated year up to real time). Every price after the starting snapshot, in both modes, is generated by a disclosed random-walk model — not real market data — and will diverge from what the real security actually does. All cash, positions and plans are saved locally in your browser under the player code you choose at startup (nothing is sent anywhere); the same code brings your game back any time you return in this same browser — it won't carry progress to a different computer or browser, and clearing site data erases it. Do not use this tool, or anything in it, to make real investment decisions about real securities.

Before you start

This is a paper-trading education sandbox, not a real brokerage, and nothing in it is investment, legal or tax advice.

Real Companies mode starts from real tickers and real starting prices/metrics, and always runs in real time. But every price move after that starting point — in both Real Companies and Super Simulator modes — is generated by a disclosed random-walk simulation, not real market data. It will diverge from what the real security actually does, sometimes within minutes.

Never use this tool, or anything in it, to make real investment decisions about real securities. All data stays in your browser's local storage, under the player code you chose — nothing is sent anywhere.
