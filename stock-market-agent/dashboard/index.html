How this is built. Prices, growth, dividend, beta and short-interest figures are pulled from free public sources (search-grounded snapshots today; the checked-in run.py agent uses yfinance + RSS feeds when run with normal internet access). Growth and Next-Gen Growth are ranked by an explicit conviction tier (1–5), not a blended score, because their underlying metrics use incompatible units (revenue growth vs. price momentum vs. analyst upside). Stability and Short Interest use a single consistent metric, so those two get a real normalized 0–100 score. Coverage spans 10 GICS sectors, — current Fortune 100 companies, and 13 next-gen growth niches (quantum computing, space, gene editing, AI, cybersecurity, EV & battery tech, fintech, clean energy, and more). A separate Funds & Company Size desk sorts 16 ETFs, mutual funds, and small/mid-cap stocks from lowest to highest risk, so you can see diversification's effect on volatility side by side — in both Real Companies and Super Simulator mode in the Simulator. This is not financial advice — it's a research starting point; verify anything before acting on it.
